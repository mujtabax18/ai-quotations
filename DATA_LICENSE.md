Quotation content should only be redistributed when you have the right to publish it.
Storing a quotation in this repository does not create ownership of third-party text.

Recommended `source` values:

- `ai`
- `manual`
- `public-domain`

Before publishing the starter dataset publicly, review manually supplied quotations
